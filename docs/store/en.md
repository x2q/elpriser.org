# en — English (source text for all other languages)

Store listing for Elpriser 1.1.0, the Europe release. The limits are the stores' own and are checked by `npm run test:store`.
Each `###` heading is a field; the text below it, up to the next heading, is the value. Do not add other headings inside a field.

## App Store (iOS)

### ios.name
Elpriser – hourly power prices

### ios.subtitle
Spot prices across Europe

### ios.promo
Today's and tomorrow's electricity spot price, hour by hour, in 30 European countries. In Denmark: your real price with grid tariff, taxes and VAT. Free, no ads.

### ios.keywords
electricity,power,price,spot,hourly,energy,kWh,tariff,Nord Pool,day-ahead,smart home,EV charging

### ios.whatsnew
Elpriser now covers Europe. Choose your country and price zone and see the electricity spot price hour by hour, today and tomorrow. The app is now available in 26 languages. In Denmark nothing changes: your grid company, tariffs, taxes and VAT work as before.

### ios.description
Elpriser shows what electricity costs right now, hour by hour, and when it gets cheaper.

EUROPE
Choose your country and price zone: 30 countries from Norway to Portugal and from Ireland to Greece, including the Western Balkans. The app shows the day-ahead spot price for today and tomorrow in cent per kWh (øre, öre or grosz where that is the local sub-unit), with the cheapest and the most expensive hour. For the Nordic zones and the Netherlands there is also an open-model forecast for the coming days.

YOUR LANGUAGE
Danish, English, German, French, Dutch, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Czech, Hungarian, Slovenian, Spanish, Portuguese, Slovak, Greek, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Serbian, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Macedonian and Albanian. Numbers, dates and currency follow your language.

DENMARK: YOUR REAL PRICE
Choose your grid company (or let the app find it from your position, used once and never stored) and the price includes grid tariff, Energinet tariffs, electricity tax and VAT, like on your bill. See exactly how the price of every hour is made up.

SMART HOME
See the cheapest hours for a heat pump, an electric car and a water heater, how much you save, and copy the plan to Home Assistant or Shelly.

AND
• 2 or 5 days ahead; tap or drag the chart to see the price of every hour
• For iPhone and iPad, light and dark mode
• No ads, no account

Prices are wholesale spot prices (day-ahead market) without taxes, grid fees and supplier margin, except in Denmark where taxes and tariffs are added. Data: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, Energy-Charts.info (Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, CC BY 4.0, converted to hourly averages by elpriser.org), Energi Data Service. The forecast model is open on Hugging Face.

Elpriser is an independent service and is not affiliated with any grid company, energy supplier or market operator. Prices are indicative; your actual settlement may differ.

Privacy policy: https://elpriser.org/privatliv (in Danish). Contact: hello@elpriser.org
