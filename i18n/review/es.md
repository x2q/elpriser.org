# es (Spanish, Spain) — terms to have checked by a native speaker

Register: informal "tú" throughout (typical for consumer apps in Spain).

| Key | My choice | Alternative | Why unsure |
|---|---|---|---|
| hero_label | "Precio de la luz ahora" | "Precio de la electricidad ahora" | "la luz" is what people say in Spain; "electricidad" is more formal. |
| mode_spot_price, part_spot_* | "Precio spot" | "Precio mayorista", "Precio del mercado diario" | "spot" is understood but "mercado diario" is the Spanish market term; "mayorista" is used only in caption_zone. |
| verdict_mid / legend_mid | "Medio" | "Normal", "Intermedio" | "Normal" may sound more natural as a price band. |
| caption_net, part_grid, place_net_note | "tarifa de red" | "peaje de acceso", "peajes y cargos" | Spanish bills split "peajes y cargos" and "costes de distribución"; "tarifa de red" is a descriptive stand-in for the Danish nettarif. |
| part_system / part_transmission | "Tarifa de sistema" / "Tarifa de transporte" (Energinet) | "Cargo del sistema", "Peaje de transporte" | Danish regulatory terms with no exact equivalent. |
| part_duty | "Impuesto sobre la electricidad" | "Impuesto especial sobre la electricidad" | The Spanish tax is "Impuesto Especial sobre la Electricidad"; I kept it generic because the Danish tax is a different one. |
| grid company (many keys) | "distribuidora" | "empresa distribuidora", "operador de red" | In Spain the grid company is the "distribuidora" (e.g. on the bill), but it can be confused with the retailer ("comercializadora"). |
| day_tonight | "esta noche" | "esta tarde" | Source means "this evening"; 21:00 prices are typically "esta noche" in Spain. |
| tile_cheapest / tile_priciest | "Más barata hoy" / "Más cara hoy" (feminine, hour implied) | "Hora más barata" | Gender agrees with "hora", but the noun is not on the tile. |
| cheaper_later | "Más barato mañana a las 03:00 — …" | "Baja mañana a las 03:00" | Masculine "barato" refers to the price; unsure about flow. |
| date_day_month, date_long | "17 oct", "Domingo 17 oct" | "17 de oct.", "domingo, 17 de oct." | Spanish normally writes "de" and a dot after abbreviations; I dropped both for space. |
| months_short | "ene … dic", no dot, "sep" | "sept.", "ene." | RAE allows "sep." or "sept."; the dot is omitted. |
| unit_ct_kwh / unit_ct | "c€/kWh", "c€" | "cént./kWh", "céntimos" | "c€/kWh" is used by CNMC/energy comparison sites; "cént." is the traditional abbreviation. |
| a11y_device_toggle | "Automatización de %1$s" | "%1$s automático" | Screen-reader wording. |
| device_water | "Calentador de agua" | "Termo eléctrico" | "termo" is the usual word for the electric tank in Spain; "calentador" may suggest a gas boiler. |
| nav_smart | "Hogar inteligente" | "Casa inteligente", "Domótica" | Domótica is the established word but may be too technical for a tab label. |
| about / location texts | "ubicación" for position | "posición" | "ubicación" is the standard phone term. |
| Store keywords (es.md) | "precio luz, spot, mercado diario, domótica" | "PVPC", "tarifa regulada" | PVPC is the most-searched Spanish term but the app does not show PVPC, so it is deliberately left out. |
