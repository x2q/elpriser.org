# hr — terms to have checked by a native speaker

Register: formal "Vi" in sentences ("Odaberite", "Provjerite"), short singular imperative on buttons and screen-reader actions ("Pokušaj ponovno", "Zatvori", "Kopiraj"). Many modern Croatian apps use informal "ti" throughout; owner's call.
