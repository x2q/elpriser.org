# sq (Albanian) — terms to have checked by a native speaker

LOW CONFIDENCE (tier 3). The whole file, and the store listing, should be reviewed by a native speaker before release. Register: formal plural ("ju": zgjidhni, juaj).

| Key | My choice | Alternative | Why unsure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Register | formal plural "ju" (zgjidhni, tuaj) | informal "ti" (zgjidh, yt) | Albanian apps use both; I used the polite plural imperative throughout, including buttons (Provoni, Kapërceni, Mbyllni, Kopjoni); native speakers may find it heavy on buttons. |
| hero_label etc. | "Çmimi i rrymës" | "Çmimi i energjisë elektrike" | "rrymë" is colloquial and common; "energji elektrike" is formal and longer. Used "energjisë elektrike" only in the tax row. |
| mode_spot_price | Çmimi i bursës | Çmimi spot | Albania now has a power exchange (ALPEX); "çmimi i bursës" is clear, "spot" may be used by traders. |
| unit_ct_kwh / unit_ct | `cent/kWh`, `cent` | `ct/kWh`, `qindarkë`, `centë` | "cent" is used in spoken Albanian; plural/definite forms were avoided. |
